Services

What we offer

Five services, from buying a car in our showroom to finding one we don't have yet. The steps below show what happens after you get in touch.

01

Vehicle Sales

Every listing shows its price (or "Price on request"), condition, mileage and availability, so you can compare cars before you visit.

02

Test Drives

Request a test drive of any available vehicle at our San Fernando showroom. Send your name, phone, email, the vehicle, and a preferred date and time. A team member then confirms a time with you by phone or email. Until they do, nothing is booked.

Request a test drive
03

Vehicle Sourcing

Looking for something outside our current inventory? Our team can source specific makes, models, and trims on your behalf.

04

Trade-In Consultation

Bringing a vehicle to trade? Tell us its make, model and year, and we'll go through its value with you as part of your purchase.

05

Vehicle Consultation

Not sure what fits your needs? Sit down with a consultant to talk through use case, budget, and category before you decide.

How it works

From first message to next steps

  1. 1

    Inquiry

    Use the contact form to choose a general inquiry, test drive, trade-in or sourcing request.

  2. 2

    Consultation

    A consultant gets in touch to talk through how you'll use the car, your budget and which category fits.

  3. 3

    Viewing

    You see the shortlisted vehicles at the showroom. For available cars, a test drive can be arranged once the team confirms a time.

  4. 4

    Next steps

    If you want to go ahead, we discuss price, any trade-in and paperwork. There is no obligation at any earlier step.

This describes the intended service flow for this demonstration site. Confirmation times and paperwork requirements have not been set.
