About this page. Apex Motors is a fictional dealership. The sections below describe the service approach this demo is designed around. They are not claims about a real business, and the details would need to be confirmed by the owner before real use.

How vehicles are selected

  • Category fit. Cars sit in one of four categories: Supercar, Track Weapon, Grand Tourer or Muscle.
  • Clear condition. Each car is listed as brand new or pre-owned. Pre-owned cars show their mileage.
  • Honest availability. A car is marked Available, Reserved or Sold, and sold cars stay visible so you can ask for something similar.

What is checked before listing

  • Specifications. Figures are compared with the manufacturer's published data and shown in one set of units: hp, Nm, km/h, kg.
  • Gaps are left blank. If a figure can't be confirmed, it is left out rather than estimated.
  • Photos. A listing photo should show the model and generation sold. Where it does not, the page says so.

What a consultation looks like

  • Questions first. We ask how you'll drive the car, what you drive now and your budget range.
  • A short list. You get a few matching cars from the inventory, or a sourcing request if none match.
  • No pressure to decide. A viewing or test drive request doesn't commit you to buy.

What happens next

See the four steps on the services page: inquiry, consultation, viewing and next steps.
